       MEET THE ORACLE

      Old soul.
      A new kind of aaram.

      Baba Aramdev is a fictional, mechanical wellness oracle. His fortunes are invitations to practise small everyday habits, not literal predictions.

      This version uses clearly labelled, prewritten demo fortunes. Gemini can power tailored replies once its server connection is configured.

      Questions aren’t saved by this app. With Gemini enabled, your question is sent to Google to generate your reply. Save a fortune only if you want it stored in this browser. Voice input uses your browser’s speech service.

      Baba can’t diagnose conditions, recommend medicines, or replace a clinician. If you feel seriously unwell or unsafe, seek urgent local help.
